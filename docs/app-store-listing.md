# App Store Listing for Doudou

## Short Description
A beautiful, privacy-focused music player for your personal Jellyfin media server.

## Full Description

**Doudou - Your Personal Music Experience**

Transform your Jellyfin media server into a beautiful mobile music experience with Doudou. Designed with privacy and simplicity in mind, Doudou provides seamless access to your personal music library without collecting any user data.

**✨ Features:**
• **Beautiful Interface** - Clean, intuitive design inspired by modern music apps
• **Full Jellyfin Integration** - Browse albums, artists, playlists, and songs
• **Advanced Audio** - Smart crossfade, gapless playback, and preloading for smooth listening
• **Background Playback** - Keep your music playing while using other apps
• **Queue Management** - Add, remove, and reorder tracks in your playbook queue
• **Playlist Support** - Create and manage playlists directly from the app
• **Favorites** - Mark and easily access your favorite tracks
• **Smart Restart** - Back button intelligently restarts current song or goes to previous
• **Offline Ready** - Automatic caching for smooth playback

**🔒 Privacy First:**
• Zero data collection - we collect absolutely nothing
• No analytics, tracking, or advertisements
• Your music stays between you and your server
• Open source and transparent

**📱 Requirements:**
• Personal Jellyfin media server (version 10.8+)
• Network connection to your server
• Android 5.0+ (API level 21)

**🎵 Perfect For:**
• Music enthusiasts with personal media libraries
• Users who value privacy and data ownership
• Anyone seeking a clean, modern Jellyfin music client
• Families sharing a home media server

**Getting Started:**
1. Set up your Jellyfin media server
2. Download Doudou and enter your server details
3. Start enjoying your music with a beautiful, private experience

**Note:** This app requires a self-hosted Jellyfin server. We do not provide media content or hosting services.
