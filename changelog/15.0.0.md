- YouTube Music home page shows more: curated sections (albums, playlists, tracks) from the service appear below Your artists.

- YouTube Music: favorites now stored locally (like SoundCloud); toggleFavorite saves/removes favorites in SharedPreferences; getStarredTracks returns local favorites; tracks show isFavorite flag based on local favorites.
